Questions

Guardrails and sources.

Who bills Medicare, and who directs care?

The billing practitioner directs care and bills. The doctor obtains consent at an initiating visit, approves the care plan, and provides general supervision. Carepak pharmacists do the monthly work as contracted clinical staff.

How is Carepak paid?

Through a contracted service fee set at fair market value. The fee is not tied to prescriptions, and it is separate from dispensing revenue.

What has to be documented?

A care plan is required, and every month needs real, documented clinical minutes. Our time logs and notes go into the chart.

What compliance review happens before launch?

Anti-Kickback and state rules apply. Every arrangement gets legal review before it starts.

Where do the numbers come from?

20.3% readmission: Medicare multiple-chronic-condition cohort, ACG definition (PMC5397355). 10+ conditions = 8.9% of patients and 50.2% of readmissions: Berkowitz & Anderson, J Hosp Med 2013. 2,334 hospitals: CMS HRRP FY2027. $15,200: AHRQ HCUP Statistical Brief #278 (2018). 2026 rates: CMS Physician Fee Schedule, national non-facility. 3.4%: J Am Geriatr Soc 2024 (2019 data). ~12,000 doctors: KFF Health News, 2021 data.
