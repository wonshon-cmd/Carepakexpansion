How to use Loan Floor

Practice on made-up loans before the real run. The left side is a practice copy of the bank panel: highlight the $ amounts and copy them for real. The right side is the tool you will use. The coach at the top ticks off each step as you do it.

Tip for the real bank panel: Ctrl+click (Mac: ⌘+click) an application to open it in a new tab. Keep the list in one tab and work each application in its own tab. When it is done, Ctrl+W (Mac: ⌘+W) closes that tab.
