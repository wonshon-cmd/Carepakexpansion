What is CCM?

A monthly check-in. Medicare pays for it.

CCM

Chronic Care Management. Patients with 2+ chronic conditions. 20+ minutes a month: care plan, med review, calls, coordination with the doctor.

BHI

Behavioral Health Integration. Depression, anxiety and more. Monthly check-ins with simple depression and anxiety screenings.

♥ Patients love it

Most elderly, medically complex patients love getting a phone call.
